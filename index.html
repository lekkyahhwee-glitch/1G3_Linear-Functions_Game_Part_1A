Fun Fact: You Spotted a Phishing Attempt!

Phishing is when someone pretends to be trustworthy — a company, a prize, a friend — to trick you into handing over personal info, money, or access to your device. It works by creating urgency or excitement that makes people act before they stop to think.

You just avoided one, but phishing shows up in lots of everyday disguises. Real examples people fall for all the time:

  • 🎁 "You've won a prize!" — a countdown timer and a link to "claim it" before time runs out
  • 📦 "Your package couldn't be delivered" — a text asking you to "reschedule" by entering your address or card details
  • 🔒 "Your account will be suspended" — a fake email urging you to "verify" your password immediately
  • 💼 "Easy money, work from home!" — a job offer that asks you to pay a small "starter fee" upfront
  • 👤 A message from a "friend" whose account was actually hacked, asking to borrow money or click a link

The giveaways are almost always the same: urgency, requests for money or personal info, strange links, and offers that feel too good to be true. You just proved you know how to spot them.
